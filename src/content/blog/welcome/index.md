This is the new home for my research notes and write-ups. Expect posts on
synthetic aperture radar, complex-valued and phase-aware deep learning, and
adapting geospatial foundation models to new sensors.

Two write-ups are already in the queue:

- **The L-band gap** — why C-band-pretrained geospatial foundation models don't
  transfer zero-shot to NISAR, and how to close the gap.
- **GuacamayaSAR** — a SAR companion for deforestation monitoring when the Amazon
  is under cloud.

The whole site is open-source on
[GitHub](https://github.com/josiahwsmith10/josiahwsmith10.github.io) — so posts
can carry real math and code right alongside the prose. More soon.
