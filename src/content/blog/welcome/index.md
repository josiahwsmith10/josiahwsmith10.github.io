This is the new home for my research notes and write-ups. The throughline is the
same idea that has run through my work since the Ph.D.: rather than treating
machine learning as a black box, fuse it with the physics of the sensor. Expect
posts on synthetic aperture radar, complex-valued and phase-aware deep learning,
and adapting geospatial foundation models to new sensors.

Two write-ups are already in the queue:

- **The L-band gap** — every public geospatial foundation model was pretrained on
  C-band SAR, but NISAR is L-band. Where does zero-shot transfer break, and how do
  we close the gap?
- **GuacamayaSAR** — a SAR companion for deforestation monitoring when the Amazon
  is under cloud.

The whole site is open-source on
[GitHub](https://github.com/josiahwsmith10/josiahwsmith10.github.io), so posts
can carry real math and code alongside the prose. More soon.
